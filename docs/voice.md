# How the website talks

Who reads this site: a parent comparing primary schools, often on a phone, often between other things.
They want answers, not a mood. Write for that person.

## Rules

1. **Answer a question a parent would ask.** Every section should answer one: what time does school end,
   how much does it cost, how much Qur'an will my child memorise, how big are the classes, who teaches.
   If a section answers nothing, cut it.
2. **Facts beat adjectives.** "Pulang pukul 14.30" says more than "jadwal yang seimbang".
   "18 siswa per kelas" says more than "perhatian personal".
3. **No slogans that any other school could use.** Test: swap in another school's name. If the sentence
   still works, rewrite it or delete it.
4. **If the fact is missing, leave a marked gap** (`[ISI]` / `[FILL]`). Never fill it with a vague phrase.
5. **Buttons say where they go.** "Lihat rincian biaya", not "Selengkapnya".
6. **Plain words, short sentences.** Use "adalah", "punya", "ada". Avoid "menjadi wadah", "senantiasa
   berkomitmen", "mewujudkan generasi", "perjalanan".
7. **Islamic terms stay as they are** (tahfidz, murajaah, adab, BTQ). In English, add a short gloss once.
8. **Sentence case for headings.** "Biaya pendidikan", not "Biaya Pendidikan Kami Yang Terjangkau".
9. **No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji.**

## Words and phrases to avoid

| Avoid | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| Menumbuhkan generasi berilmu dan berakhlak mulia | What the school actually does each day |
| Fondasi kuat untuk dunia dan akhirat | A specific programme and its target |
| Mulai perjalanan bersama kami | Daftar / Jadwalkan kunjungan |
| Program unggulan | The programme's name |
| Selengkapnya | Where the link goes: "Baca sambutan lengkap" |
| Berkomitmen untuk memberikan yang terbaik | Delete |
| Lingkungan belajar yang nyaman dan kondusif | Describe the room, the class size, the schedule |

## Visual rules that go with the copy

- No decorative icons. If an icon doesn't tell you something, remove it.
- No vanity numbers ("1000+ alumni"). Show numbers parents use to decide: fees, hours, class size, targets.
- Real photos of the real school only. No stock photos of children.
- Everything important is visible without clicking: no auto-rotating sliders.
